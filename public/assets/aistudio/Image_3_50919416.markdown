## Brand & Style

The design system merges prestigious scientific journalism with the quiet drama of deep space exploration. Its identity is forged at the crossroads of classic high-end publication design and aerospace telemetry interfaces. It evokes intellectual curiosity, awe, and empirical rigor—treating astronomical data not as cold readouts, but as modern artifacts of human discovery.

The design philosophy unites **Dark Minimalist Editorial** with **Precision HUD / Glassmorphism**:
- **Immersive Void:** Grounded in absolute black and cosmic charcoal surfaces that push deep-space astronomical photography and 3D celestial models into high-contrast focus.
- **Editorial Authority:** Grand, classic display typography sets an authoritative, literary tone, contrasted against razor-sharp technical mono and grotesk figures.
- **Instrument Precision:** Fine hair-width dividers, glowing telemetry badges, and translucent glass panels create the tactile sensation of an observatory cockpit or specialized mission console.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for deep-space visualization in a native dark environment:
- **Primary (`#FFCC00`):** The unmistakable signature gold/yellow border of discovery. Reserved for primary calls to action, high-priority telemetry highlights, focal indicators, and active navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#0057FF`):** Deep orbital beacon blue. Applied to secondary controls, orbital path vectors, planetary trajectory highlights, and active scientific telemetry.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A800`):** Muted solar amber used for secondary warnings, intermediate states, and hover transitions on primary elements.
- **Neutral (`#121417`):** Midnight graphite base. Serves as the atmospheric ground plane, preventing harsh pure-black eye strain while retaining boundless depth.

### Functional Application
- **Surfaces:** Stacked from `space-void` (`#000000`) for canvas bases, up through `surface-slate` (`#0D1117`) and `surface-charcoal` (`#191919`) for elevated panels and metric cards.
- **Outlines & Separators:** Strictly use `border-subtle` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) to yield razor-sharp, low-noise grid lines reminiscent of precision reticles.
- **Telemetry Indicators:** `telemetry-cyan` (`#00E5FF`) for Earth-relative coordinates and cryogenic data; `telemetry-mars` (`#FF453A`) for atmospheric alerts and trajectory risks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dramatic tension between classical exploratory literature and mission control instruments:
- **Headlines & Feature Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes the historic legacy of editorial storytelling and celestial atlases. Large titles must feature tight leading with slight negative tracking to preserve weight and gravitas.
- **Narrative & Contextual Copy (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a crisp, geometric sans-serif bridge between literary titles and raw telemetry. Used across long-form analysis, overview synopses, and descriptive panels.
- **Instruments, Metrics & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Strictly reserved for numerical telemetry, spectral classifications, orbital metrics, coordinates, and micro-labels. Always rendered with tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain exact alignment across comparative data matrices.

Use `label-caps` in uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.14em`) for category anchors, axis badges, and scientific prefixes (e.g., `APOAPSIS // 405,400 KM`).
